Seasonal tendencies

ICT's seasonal lessons use forex and US data. For India, the engine tests NIFTY's own monthly returns and only uses them if the evidence is strong.

explained · code comingModule M13
Exact rule in the engine
For each calendar month: mean return, hit rate, t-stat over 10+ years
Seasonal filter allowed only if |t-stat| ≥ bias.seasonality_min_tstat (2.0)

The two years of data included here are far too few. Add a long NIFTY history CSV in data/raw/ and the study becomes meaningful.

Common mistakes
  • Applying US seasonal patterns to NIFTY.
  • Believing a pattern based on 5 data points.
Exercise

Get 15+ years of NIFTY monthly closes. Which months have a t-stat above 2? Probably none, and that is a useful finding.
