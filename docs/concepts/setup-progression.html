Setup progression

ICT's 'Trade Conditions and Setup Progressions': the order in which conditions must line up. Each step narrows the trade down.

explained · code comingModule M6–M7

The five steps

  1. Bias (weekly). Up, down or range, written as one sentence. E.g. 'Weekly is in discount after sweeping the April low, drawing to the open ITH at 24,774.'
  2. Location. Price is at a weekly/daily PD array, in discount for longs (premium for shorts).
  3. Liquidity. A pool has just been swept, or there is a clear untouched pool as the target.
  4. Confirmation. A daily (or lower-TF for timing) MSS by close.
  5. Entry. The FVG / OB / OTE left by the MSS move, with a stop beyond the sweep and a target at the next pool.

The chart above is the whole progression on real 15-minute data, found automatically by the engine. Steps 1 and 2 come from the daily/weekly charts.

Common mistakes
  • Starting at step 5 (seeing an FVG) and working backwards to justify it.
  • Skipping the written bias. If you cannot write it in one sentence, you do not have one.
Exercise

Take one past NIFTY swing move and write the five steps for it in your journal, with dates and prices.
