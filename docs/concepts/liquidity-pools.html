Concept 4 of 25 · Part A: Where is the liquidity?

Liquidity pools

Liquidity collects where many traders put stops at the same place. Those clusters are the market's targets.

explained · code comingModule M2

The idea

Retail traders are taught that a double top is 'strong resistance'. So they short under it and put their stops just above it, while breakout traders put buy-stops there too. The result is a dense pool of buy orders a few points above the equal highs. ICT teaches that price is engineered towards such pools.

Types of pool

PoolWhere the stops are
Equal highs / relative equal highsJust above them (buy-side)
Equal lowsJust below them (sell-side)
Cluster of open swing pointsBeyond the outermost one
Previous day / week / month high and lowBeyond each; watched by everyone
Old ITH / LTH (open float)Beyond it; larger than short-term ones
Exact rule in the engine
Equal: two or more swing prices within max(equal_tolerance_pct × price, equal_tolerance_atr × ATR)
Rank score = 0.4 × closeness + 0.35 × swing tier + 0.25 × touches   (config: liquidity.rank_weights)
Draw on liquidity = highest-ranked pool in the direction of bias

How to use it

  1. Mark the pools above and below price.
  2. Ask: which one is price more likely to reach first, given the weekly bias?
  3. That pool is your target. The pool on the other side is where a sweep could set up a reversal.
Common mistakes
  • Placing your own stop exactly at the obvious equal low. You become the liquidity.
  • Treating 'equal' too strictly: relative equal highs a few points apart work the same way.
Exercise

For RELIANCE, HDFCBANK and INFY, mark one pool above and one below today's price. Write down which you expect first and why, then check in two weeks.
