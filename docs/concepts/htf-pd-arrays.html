Concept 12 of 25 · Part B: Where will price react?

HTF PD arrays

'Premium/discount arrays' is ICT's umbrella term for every zone price reacts to. On weekly and daily charts they are your trade locations.

explained · code comingModule M3

The idea

Instead of hunting for setups everywhere, a swing trader builds a map once a week: all the higher-timeframe zones above and below price. Then they wait for price to reach one of them. Everything else is noise.

The arrays, ranked roughly by ICT's order

Exact rule in the engine
Every zone stores: timeframe, top, bottom, created date, respected_before (bool),
age in bars (dropped after zone_max_age_bars), and a one-line 'reason it matters'
Common mistakes
  • Drawing so many zones that price is always 'at a zone'. Keep only fresh, displacement-created ones.
  • Mixing 15m zones into a weekly map.
Exercise

Every Sunday, mark 3 weekly zones above and 3 below NIFTY, with a one-line reason each. That is your plan for the week.
