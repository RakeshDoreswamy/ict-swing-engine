Concept 11 of 25 · Part B: Where will price react?

OTE: optimal trade entry

After an impulse leg, the best-value pullback is the 62 to 79% retracement, with 70.5% as the sweet spot. It is deep in discount, near the stop and far from the target.

explained · code comingModule M4

The idea

OTE combines premium/discount with Fibonacci. A pullback that only reaches 30% is too shallow (bad R:R); one beyond 79% is at risk of becoming a full reversal. The 62 to 79% band balances fill probability and reward.

Exact rule in the engine
For an up leg (low → high): OTE = high − [0.62, 0.79] × (high − low); sweet spot 0.705
For a down leg: low + [0.62, 0.79] × (high − low)
Config: premium_discount.ote_low / ote_high / ote_sweet

R:R arithmetic

Leg 100 → 200. Buy at 70.5% = 129.5, stop below 100 at 98: risk 31.5. Target the leg's high, 200: reward 70.5. R:R ≈ 2.2. Buying the same leg at 50% (150) would give risk 52 and reward 50: under 1.

Common mistakes
  • Drawing the fib on a leg that did not displace.
  • Using OTE without a reason the level should hold (FVG/OB inside the band).
Exercise

On 3 historical NIFTY swing moves, draw the fib on the impulse leg and mark whether price reached OTE before continuing.
