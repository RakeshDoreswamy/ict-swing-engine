Concept 8 of 25 · Part B: Where will price react?

Order block

A bullish order block is the last down-close candle before a strong move up: in ICT's reading, where the big buy programme was loaded.

explained · code comingModule M3

The idea

Institutions cannot fill a large order in one candle. The last down candle before the explosion is where they absorbed sellers. If price returns there, ICT argues they defend it, adding to positions near their average price. The 50% level of the candle, the mean threshold, should not be closed through.

Exact rule in the engine
Bullish OB: the last down-close candle within ob_lookback_bars before a bullish displacement candle
Bearish OB: the last up-close candle before a bearish displacement candle
Zone = that candle's [low, high]; mean threshold = 50%
Invalid (becomes a breaker) when a later bar CLOSES beyond the far side

Quality checklist

  • Did the move away displace and leave an FVG?
  • Did it break a swing (structure)?
  • Is it in discount (bull OB) or premium (bear OB)?
  • Is it fresh, i.e. not yet revisited?
Common mistakes
  • Picking any down candle. It must be followed by displacement.
  • Ignoring the mean threshold: a close below the OB's 50% is a warning.
Exercise

For 3 large caps, find the bullish OB under the most recent rally on the daily chart. Did price return to it? Did the mean threshold hold?
