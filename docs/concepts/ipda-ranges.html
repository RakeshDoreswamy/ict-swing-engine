Concept 19 of 25 · Part E: Slow context

IPDA data ranges

ICT's Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm idea: price refers back to the highs and lows of the last 20, 40 and 60 trading days.

explained · code comingModule M5

How to read it

  • The 20-day range is short-term context: where is price inside the last month?
  • The 40 and 60-day highs/lows are the next liquidity targets beyond the short-term range.
  • When price is in the lower part of the 60-day range with a bullish weekly bias, the 60-day high is a reasonable draw.
Exact rule in the engine
For N in [20, 40, 60]: high/low of the N bars BEFORE today (today excluded, no lookahead)
Common mistakes
  • Treating the 60-day high as a precise reversal level. It is a target, not a wall.
Exercise

Each Monday, write NIFTY's 20/40/60-day high and low and note which ones price reached by Friday.
