Concept 5 of 25 · Part A: Where is the liquidity?

Liquidity sweep (stop hunt)

Price pokes beyond a pool, triggers the stops, then reverses. The wick takes the liquidity; the close rejects the level.

explained · code comingModule M2

The idea

Large players need the other side to fill big orders. To buy a lot, they need many sellers, and the stops under a low are exactly that: sell orders. Running price through the low fills the big buy order against those stops. Once filled, there is nothing left to push price lower, so it snaps back. That is the sweep.

Exact rule in the engine
Sell-side sweep at bar j: low[j] < open swing low  AND  close[j] > that swing low
Buy-side sweep at bar j:  high[j] > open swing high AND close[j] < that swing high
Only swings confirmed before bar j count.

Sweep vs breakout

SweepBreakout
CloseBack inside the levelBeyond the level
Next barsMove away, often fastHold beyond, retest
MeaningLiquidity taken, reversal likelyLevel genuinely broken

A sweep alone is not a trade. It becomes one when it is followed by a market structure shift.

Common mistakes
  • Buying the moment price wicks below a low, before the close. Many 'sweeps' become breakdowns.
  • Ignoring which level was swept: an LTL sweep matters more than a minor STL.
Exercise

Find 3 NIFTY daily bars that wicked below an old swing low and closed back above it. Record what happened over the next 10 bars.
