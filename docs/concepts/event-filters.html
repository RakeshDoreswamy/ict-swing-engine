Event and liquidity filters

Scheduled events can move price past your stop before you can react. The engine refuses entries when one falls inside the expected holding period.

explained · code comingModule M11

Filters

FilterRule
EarningsNo entry if the company reports within the expected holding period
RBI policyNo new index/bank entries in the days around an MPC decision
Union BudgetNo entries across Budget day
Index rebalancingAvoid affected stocks around NSE rebalance dates
Turnover20-day average daily turnover ≥ min_avg_turnover_cr
Gap riskFlag stocks whose average overnight gap > gap_risk_avg_gap_pct
Exact rule in the engine
Events come from data/events/events.csv: date, type, symbol (or ALL)
Holding period estimate = time_stop_bars
Common mistakes
  • Assuming the stop protects you on a result day.
  • Forgetting that event dates change; update the calendar CSV monthly from NSE/RBI announcements.
