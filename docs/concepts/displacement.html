Concept 6 of 25 · Part B: Where will price react?

Displacement

Displacement is a large-bodied candle that moves price decisively. It creates FVGs, defines order blocks and confirms structure shifts.

explained · code comingModule M3

The idea

Normal candles overlap and drift. When a large player commits, price moves so fast that one candle covers ground several normal candles would. ICT reads that energy as intent. Without displacement, an FVG is weak, an order block is just a candle, and a structure break is just noise.

Exact rule in the engine
Displacement candle: |close − open| > displacement_atr_mult × ATR(14) of the PRIOR bars
Default multiplier: 1.2 for zones (pd_arrays), 1.0 for MSS (confirmation)

What displacement tells you

  • Direction: the side institutions are pushing.
  • The candle before it is the order block.
  • The gap it leaves is the FVG.
  • When it closes through a swing, it is an MSS.
Common mistakes
  • Calling a big candle on a high-volatility day displacement. Measure against ATR, not your eye.
  • Chasing the displacement candle itself. Wait for the pullback into what it left behind.
Exercise

On NIFTY 15m, find the 5 largest-bodied candles of the last week. For each, mark the candle before it (order block) and any gap it left (FVG).
