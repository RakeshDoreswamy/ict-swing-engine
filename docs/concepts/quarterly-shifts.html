Concept 20 of 25 · Part E: Slow context

Quarterly shifts

ICT observes that markets tend to change their dominant direction roughly every quarter. The engine detects it as a close through the prior 60-day range in the opposite direction.

explained · code comingModule M5
Exact rule in the engine
Shift up at bar i: close[i] > max(high of prior 60 bars) AND the last shift was down
Shift down mirrors it. Used only as a slow bias layer.

This is the slowest filter in the engine. It does not time entries; it tells you which side of the market to favour for the next few months.

Common mistakes
  • Expecting shifts on a calendar schedule. 'Quarterly' is approximate.

Mark the shifts on NIFTY weekly over the last 5 years. How regular are they?
