Concept 10 of 25 · Part B: Where will price react?

Premium, discount and equilibrium

Split the current dealing range in half. The top half is premium (expensive), the bottom half is discount (cheap). The middle is equilibrium.

explained · code comingModule M4

The idea

Institutions are price-sensitive buyers. ICT's rule is simple: in an uptrend, buy only in discount; in a downtrend, sell only in premium. This one filter removes most bad entries: chasing breakouts at the top of a range.

Exact rule in the engine
Dealing range = most recent confirmed intermediate swing low → intermediate swing high
Equilibrium (EQ) = 50%
Long entries only below EQ; short entries only above EQ

Picking the right range

  • For a daily swing trade, use the daily ITL–ITH, or the weekly range for context.
  • When a new intermediate swing confirms, the range is redrawn.
  • Nested ranges: a discount inside a weekly discount is the best location.
Common mistakes
  • Drawing the range from a tiny short-term swing, so 'discount' flips every few bars.
  • Buying in discount when the weekly bias is down. Discount in a downtrend is not cheap; it is falling.
Exercise

For NIFTY daily, draw the current dealing range and write whether price is in premium or discount. Repeat on weekly. Do they agree?
