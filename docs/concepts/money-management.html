Concept 16 of 25 · Part D: Survive and manage

Money management

Risk a fixed small fraction of capital per trade. It is the only part of trading you fully control, and it decides whether you survive a losing streak.

explained · code comingModule M9

Position size calculator

Exact rule in the engine
qty = floor( capital × risk% / (|entry − stop| × gap_slippage_factor) )
risk% = 1.0, cut to 0.5 after 3 consecutive losses or an 8% drawdown
Caps: max_open_trades 5, max_per_sector 2, max correlation 0.8
Common mistakes
  • Sizing by 'how confident I feel'.
  • Forgetting gaps: Indian stocks gap through stops on results and news; the pad factor accounts for this.
  • Three bank stocks at 1% each is 3% on one idea. Sector caps exist for this.
