R-multiples and expectancy

R is your initial risk. A trade that hits its stop is −1R; a trade that makes twice the risk is +2R. Measuring in R makes every trade comparable.

explained · code comingModule M14

Expectancy calculator

Exact rule in the engine
R = (exit − entry) / (entry − initial stop)   (sign flipped for shorts)
Expectancy = win% × avg win R − loss% × avg loss R
Graduation gate: ≥ 30 trades AND average R > 0, then start live at 25% size

Why 30 trades?

With 10 trades, a 40% win-rate strategy can easily show 20% or 70% by luck. Thirty is the minimum to say anything; 100 is better. That is why the plan is 3 months of paper trading before real money.

Common mistakes
  • Measuring in rupees, which hides that you took bigger risk on some trades.
  • Judging a strategy after 5 trades.
Exercise

Convert your last 10 trades (paper or real) to R. What is your expectancy?
