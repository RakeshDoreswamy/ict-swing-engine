Concept 17 of 25 · Part D: Survive and manage

Position trade management

Once in, the job is to protect capital first and let the winner pay. Every rule is written in advance so you do not improvise mid-trade.

explained · code comingModule M10
Exact rule in the engine
Stop to breakeven at +breakeven_at_r (1R), or when a new daily swing forms in your favour
Trail the stop under each new intermediate swing low (above each high for shorts)
Take partial_exit_pct (50%) at the first internal pool; runner to the main target
Exit on a daily close back through the MSS swing (invalidation)
Time stop: exit after time_stop_bars (25) with no progress

Why each rule exists

RuleProtects against
Breakeven at 1RA winner turning into a full loser
Trailing under swingsGiving back a large open profit
Partial at first poolRegret and emotional exits; it banks R
Invalidation closeHoping a broken setup recovers
Time stopCapital stuck in a trade that is not working
Common mistakes
  • Moving the stop to breakeven too early (before 1R), so normal pullbacks stop you out.
  • Widening the stop. Never.
